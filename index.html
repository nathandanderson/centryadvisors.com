Who we are

Executive-level consulting, with a primary emphasis on strategy, finance and accounting.

Centry Advisors helps businesses reach their full potential through expert financial and strategic guidance. Our experience spans real estate and construction, energy, mining and healthcare. That breadth lets us meet a business where it actually is, rather than where a playbook says it should be.

The firm is structured to accommodate multiple advisors who share our commitment to excellence and bring their own expertise to the table. You get senior people doing senior work, without the overhead of a full-time executive hire.

Your success is our success.

Founder

Dean Anderson, CPA

Principal & Managing Advisor

Dean is an accomplished executive with more than 25 years of experience in executive management, strategy, finance and accounting. He has served as Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer for companies across real estate and construction, energy, mining and healthcare, leading organizations through growth, restructuring, financing and operational turnarounds.

His in-depth knowledge of US GAAP and IFRS, paired with hands-on experience resolving complex technical accounting issues, means clients' reporting and compliance are always in good hands. He is fluent in Spanish and serves as Treasurer on the board of directors of Habitat for Humanity Greater Salt Lake Area. He has also served on the boards of Bike Utah and the Urban Land Institute's Utah District Council.
